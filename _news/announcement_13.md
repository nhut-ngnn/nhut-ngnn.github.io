## About the Euréka Award

The [**Euréka Award for Student Research**](https://eureka.khoahoctre.com.vn/) is a prestigious annual competition for university students in Viet Nam. It aims to:

- Encourage scientific research among undergraduates  
- Promote innovative and applicable solutions for real-world problems  
- Build a community of young researchers across universities and disciplines  

Receiving a **Consolation Prize** in the **Information Technology** field is a valuable recognition of both the **scientific contribution** and the **practical potential** of our work.
