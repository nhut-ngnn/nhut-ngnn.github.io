Our paper **Enhancing multimodal emotion recognition with dynamic fuzzy membership and attention fusion** has been accepted in the journal Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence (ISI/Scopus Q1).

We introduce FleSER, a new multimodal emotion recognition architecture that combines dynamic fuzzy membership with attention-based fusion. Unlike most existing SER models that apply fuzzy logic only at the decision level, FleSER uses a feature-level rule-based fuzzy mechanism to refine audio and text representations before fusion.

Looking forward to sharing more details at the symposium!
