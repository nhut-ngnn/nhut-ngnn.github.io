### Duc Ngoc Minh Dang

Duc mentors our research direction with a systems perspective rooted in large-scale speech and language deployments. At the Language & Knowledge Lab (HCMUT) he spearheads conversational intelligence projects that translate to real products, and he keeps our experiments grounded in data-backed rigor.

**Expertise we rely on**
- Low-resource speech recognition and understanding for Vietnamese and neighboring languages.
- Knowledge graph grounding for conversational recommendation, tutoring, and health triage.
- Benchmarking methodology that balances industrial constraints with academic novelty.

**Mentorship highlights**
- Helps us stress-test ideas with ablation plans and statistical checkpoints before we invest weeks of effort.
- Opens access to annotation guidelines, compute credits, and lab reviewers for our manuscripts.
- Coaches us on preparing demo narratives so student researchers can communicate confidently at conferences.
