<h2 class="ac-section-title">Research Focus</h2>

<div class="ac-grid">
  <div class="ac-grid-item">
    <span class="ac-grid-index">I</span>
    <h3>Multimodal Emotion Recognition</h3>
    <p>
      Cross-modal fusion across audio, text, and vision &mdash; graph attention,
      gated fusion, and adaptive attention for decoding expressive cues.
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="ac-grid-item">
    <span class="ac-grid-index">II</span>
    <h3>Speech &amp; Audio Intelligence</h3>
    <p>
      Robust representation learning for emotion, speaker traits, and conversational
      analytics on top of transformer speech encoders.
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="ac-grid-item">
    <span class="ac-grid-index">III</span>
    <h3>Human-Centered AI</h3>
    <p>
      Datasets, interfaces, and evaluation loops that keep people at the centre of
      intelligent systems and make their behaviour legible.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<h2 class="ac-section-title">Currently Exploring</h2>

<ol class="ac-enum">
  <li>
    <strong>Emotion in conversational context</strong>
    <span>
      Modelling emotional dynamics across a human&ndash;AI dialogue, so a system can
      perceive, track, and adapt to affective cues as the conversation unfolds.
    </span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Scalable graph-based multimodal architectures</strong>
    <span>
      Efficient graph and hypergraph networks for real-time inference over
      heterogeneous modalities &mdash; speech, text, and vision &mdash; in emotionally rich interactions.
    </span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Semi-supervised and contrastive learning</strong>
    <span>
      Using unlabelled multimodal data, including federated settings, to improve
      robustness and generalisation when annotation is limited.
    </span>
  </li>
</ol>
