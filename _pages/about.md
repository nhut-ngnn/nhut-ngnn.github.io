- **CemoBAM** accepted at APNOMS 2025: dual-stream heterogeneous graph + CBAM architecture that advances multimodal SER benchmarks.

- Teaching assistant and research collaborator within AITA Lab, FPT University, mentoring junior cohorts on deep learning projects.
- Curating multimodal datasets and reproducible baselines for the Vietnamese research community.
